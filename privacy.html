Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

JARVIS is a personal, single-user voice assistant that runs on the owner's own computer. This policy explains what data it handles and how. It applies to the assistant and to this website.

1. Who this covers

JARVIS is used by one person: the owner of the computer on which it is installed, using that person's own Google account. There is no public sign-up and no other users.

2. Google data that is accessed

With the owner's explicit consent through Google's OAuth screen, JARVIS may access:

  • Gmail — to read, search, label (for example mark as read) and send messages from the owner's own mailbox, and only when the owner asks it to.
  • Google Calendar — to list, create and delete events in the owner's own calendar, and only when the owner asks it to.

3. How that data is used

  • Only to carry out the owner's spoken or typed requests, e.g. reading an unread email aloud, replying to it, or adding a calendar event.
  • Content needed to answer a request may be processed by the AI model providers used by the assistant (Google Gemini and Groq) solely to generate that answer.
  • Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train third-party models by us.

4. Storage and retention

  • Google OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer and are never sent to us or to any third party.
  • Small notes the owner asks the assistant to remember (for example a birthday or a preference) are stored in a local file on the owner's computer. The owner can delete them at any time.
  • Conversation audio and text are processed in the moment to produce a reply; they are not kept on any server operated by us.

5. Sharing

No Google user data is shared with any third party, except the AI model providers listed above and strictly to generate the requested reply. No data is sold or rented.

6. Your control and deletion

  • You can revoke JARVIS' access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Access stops immediately.
  • Deleting the local token file and the local memory file removes everything JARVIS has stored.

7. Security

Credentials are kept local to the owner's machine and are excluded from any version control. Access tokens are only used over HTTPS against Google's official APIs.

8. Children

JARVIS is a personal tool and is not directed at children.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
