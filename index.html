JARVIS

A personal voice assistant, built for exactly one user: its owner.

JARVIS is a private, single-user desktop assistant. It runs on the owner's own computer and responds to the owner's spoken commands out loud. There is no sign-up, no public service and no multi-user account system.

What it does

  • Listens to spoken commands in English and Hindi, and answers with speech.
  • Reads and sends email from the owner's own Gmail account — only when the owner asks.
  • Creates, lists and deletes events in the owner's own Google Calendar.
  • Answers general questions, plays music, runs web searches and remembers details the owner chooses to tell it.

Who it is for

One person — the account owner. The assistant never accesses the data of any other person, and nothing is collected from visitors to this page.

How Google data is used

JARVIS requests access to the owner's Gmail and Google Calendar through Google's official APIs, with the owner's explicit consent. That data is used only to carry out the owner's own spoken requests — for example, reading an unread message aloud or adding a calendar event. It is never sold, never used for advertising and never shared with anyone else.
